## Test procedure

1. Obtain consent and assign a neutral ID such as T1; do not record names or emails here.
2. Give the tester the first-run guide and let them work without live coaching.
3. Ask whether they found the menu bar app, opened the dashboard, and understood a
   result or empty state. Ask them to explain remaining quota, API-equivalent cost
   and unknown readings in their own words, without copying personal dashboard data.
4. Record confusing steps and blockers below. If a step needs intervention, record
   that as an assisted outcome rather than an unassisted success.
5. Fix reproducible blockers, then repeat the affected steps and record the outcome.
   Test the DMG route when PS8 makes it available; do not claim it was tested before then.
